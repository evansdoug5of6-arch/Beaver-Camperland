Right off the freeway

Camperland sits just off I-15 in Beaver, so there's no long drive down back roads after a day behind the wheel. Pull off, check in, and hook up. It's an easy overnight stop between Salt Lake City and Las Vegas, and getting back on the road in the morning is just as quick.

Reservations are taken by phone at (435) 438-2808.

Around Beaver

Camperland makes an easy base for the Tushar Mountains and the rest of the Beaver area.

Up Beaver Canyon

Eagle Point Resort

Skiing in winter, plus lift-served mountain biking and summer events high in the Tushars.

East of town

Beaver River

Trout fishing and shady pullouts along the canyon road.

Up Beaver Canyon

Puffer Lake

A high alpine lake for fishing, paddling and cool summer afternoons.

East of town

Fishlake National Forest

Hiking, OHV trails and wildflower meadows across the Tushar range.

West of town

Minersville Reservoir

Boating and year-round fishing in a big open-sky setting.

In town

Historic Beaver

Old stone buildings, the courthouse museum, and famous local cheese curds.
